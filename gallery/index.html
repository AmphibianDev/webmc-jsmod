What converts, and what cannot
A converted Fabric jar keeps its events, keybinds, commands and modules, and its mixins are named against the compiled client and applied inside the safety valves. Heavy mods land partly: Meteor Client registers 240 plans of which ~150 apply and run; the rest are refused by name, in the Mods panel. Two things will never convert, and it is better to say so: renderer and performance mods (Iris, Sodium, Lithium — WebGL2 is not their pipeline, and native code cannot run in a tab), and native encoders such as Flashback's video export. For those, a JavaScript rewrite or a real JVM in the browser is the route, not a converter run.
A mod here is a .jsmod package or a plain JS file plus a manifest. The converter turns a Fabric jar into one; docs/API.md is the API a hand-written mod uses.
