What this is

A browser client with its own JavaScript modding API: mods load at play time from this page, a dropped file or the gallery. A converted Fabric mod keeps its events, keybinds, commands and modules; its mixins are named against the compiled client (17,000+ methods, fields, accessors) and applied inside the safety valves — a patch that hurts the client is rolled back by name.
